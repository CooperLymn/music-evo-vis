READING THE CHARTS

A note on the sample.

This is a snapshot of RateYourMusic's 5,000 most rated albums, not a census of music. The descriptors reflect community tagging, which may have happened long after an album was released. Percentages count albums, not ratings or listeners.

The 1950s contain only 37 sampled albums and the 2020s are incomplete, so the main comparison uses 1960–2019. The 15 mood labels are an explicit editorial selection; every descriptor and the preprocessing rules are documented in the project repository.
